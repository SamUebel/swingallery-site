Parking in the back lot

From the lot to the front door

The lot belongs to the Environmental Health Department and is open to us in the evening.

  1. Turn in off William P Dooley

    The lot entrance is the Environmental Health Department. For GPS, use 3841 William P Dooley Bypass.

  2. Park behind the hall

    The lot sits directly behind Liberty Exhibition Hall. You can also spot the building from the Ludlow Viaduct.

  3. Walk around to the front

    Follow the path to the left of the building and to the right of the outdoor seating area.
