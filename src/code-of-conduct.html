Code of conduct

Everyone should feel safe on the floor.

This guideline helps everyone at SwinGallery express themselves, learn, have fun and feel safe.

Being a good event attendee

  • Be mindful of others' time

    If you ask someone for a dance, great. Asking for a second dance in a row is sometimes nice, but a third is usually over the line. Let them rest, or let them ask you for the next one.

  • Be mindful of others' personal space

    Partners can end up physically closer than they're comfortable with. Being close doesn't mean giving up all personal space, or that someone is romantically interested.

    If someone is tense or nervous, take it down a notch rather than holding them tighter.

  • Be aware of those around you

    Taking up too much room or invading other dancers' space makes it less fun for everyone. Accidents happen: if you bump into someone or step on a foot, apologize and move on.

    Aerials are not allowed during social dances because of the risk of injury to you and others. Even simple lifts or dips where the follow's feet leave the floor can make follows uncomfortable. We want you to have a good time, but safety always comes first.

  • Be mindful of others' feelings

    Unsolicited advice is a major no-no. If someone asks you a question, of course help out. But few things are more embarrassing than being stopped mid-dance and told you're doing it wrong. The only exception: if someone is causing discomfort or pain, tell them directly but constructively.

    Being social doesn't mean you have to dance with everyone, and not everyone will want to dance with you. It's always nice to ask, and just as important to take "no" gracefully. If you don't want to dance, "No thanks" is always fine.

  • Be the best dancer you can be

    Take lessons. Ask questions. Listen to the music and focus on your partner.

    Hygiene and responsible behavior matter. Keep yourself clean and wear deodorant. If you're sick, stay home. If you're drunk, we'll get you a ride.

Harassment will not be tolerated

Harassment can include offensive comments, deliberate intimidation, stalking, uninvited photographing or recording, inappropriate physical contact, and unwelcome sexual attention. Harassment of any kind is grounds for removal from the event or activation of the accountability process.

If you experience harassment or feel unsafe at a dance event or with other attendees, please bring it to any SwinGallery staff member. These matters are handled with complete confidentiality.

Not comfortable raising it face to face? Email info@swingallery.org or message us on Facebook.
