Your first night

Never danced before? Perfect.

Most of our regulars walked in knowing nothing. Here's how a first Thursday goes.

  1. Arrive by 8:30

    Pay the $5 cover at the door. Wear whatever you're comfortable moving in.

  2. Take the beginner lesson

    A 30-minute crash course in East Coast Swing. We rotate partners, so you don't need to bring one.

  3. Stay for the dance

    Social dancing runs 9:00 to 11:30. Ask anyone to dance, try what you just learned, and have fun.

  • No partner required Come alone or bring friends. Partners rotate in the lesson.
  • Casual attire welcome Jeans and sneakers are fine. Vintage is fun but never required.
  • Five dances, one floor Lindy Hop, East Coast Swing, Blues, Charleston and Balboa.
  • $5 at the door Includes the beginner lesson. Live band nights are $15.
